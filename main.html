Food assistance programs

SNAP (food stamps) and WIC put money on a card for groceries every month. Here's how to apply in Oklahoma and where to get help with the paperwork.

SNAP · food stamps

Apply for SNAP

  1. Apply online at OKDHSLive. It's the fastest way, and you can save and finish later.
  2. Upload any documents they ask for, like pay stubs or ID.
  3. Oklahoma Human Services calls you for a short interview.
  4. If approved, your benefits arrive on an EBT card you use like a debit card.

Households with very little income may get expedited benefits within about 7 days. Most decisions take up to 30 days.

Double Up Oklahoma

Get more produce for your SNAP

Spend SNAP at a participating market or store and Double Up matches it dollar for dollar, up to $20 a day, for Oklahoma-grown fruits and vegetables. Everyone on SNAP qualifies automatically.

  • The Market at EastPoint (northeast OKC grocery)
  • OKC Farmers Public Market
  • Eastside Fresh Market
  • Scissortail Park Farmers Market

Farmers markets are seasonal. At a market, stop by the information booth before you shop. Double Up dollars expire 60 days after you earn them.

Learn more at Hunger Free Oklahoma

Money-saving tips

Use SNAP online

Walmart, Amazon, and Aldi (through Instacart) accept SNAP EBT for online grocery orders, which helps if you don't have a ride to the store.

Double your produce

Use SNAP where Double Up Oklahoma is offered and get matching dollars for fruits and vegetables. See the SNAP & WIC tab for OKC locations.

Shop the weekly ad

Plan meals around what's on sale this week, pick store brands, and buy rice, beans and oats in bulk. Clip digital coupons in the store's app before you check out.
